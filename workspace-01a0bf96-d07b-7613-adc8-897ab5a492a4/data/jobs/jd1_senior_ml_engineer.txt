Senior Machine Learning Engineer — Finlytics AI (Pune / Remote)

- Experience with Docker and MLOps practices to ship models to production
- 4+ years of experience in machine learning or data science roles
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering or related field

Nice to have

- NLP and large language models (LLM) exposure

Responsibilities
- Design and develop production ML models for fraud and recommendations
