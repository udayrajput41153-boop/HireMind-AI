Data Analyst — RetailWave (Ahmedabad / Hybrid)

About the role
We need a data analyst to turn retail data into insight: dashboards, cohort analysis and experimentation for category and marketing teams.

Requirements (must have)
- Advanced SQL for analysis and reporting
- Excel for quick exploration and modeling
- Power BI or Tableau dashboard development
- Statistics fundamentals and structured problem solving
- 1+ years of experience in analytics
- Bachelor's degree in any quantitative or business discipline

Nice to have
- Python for automation and deeper analysis
- A/B testing and experimentation experience
- Communication skills for stakeholder storytelling

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for sales and inventory
- Analyze campaigns with SQL and statistics, present findings
- Partner with marketing on A/B testing roadmaps
- Develop self-serve datasets for business teams
